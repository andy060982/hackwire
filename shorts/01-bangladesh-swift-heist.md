# Bangladesh Bank SWIFT Heist ($81M)

## Title
How Hackers Stole $81 Million From a Central Bank

## Hashtags

## Script

In February 2016, hackers stole 81 million dollars from the Central Bank of Bangladesh. Here's how.

They'd been inside the bank's network for over a year, studying the SWIFT payment system — the backbone of global banking.

On a Thursday night, they sent 35 fraudulent transfer requests totaling nearly a billion dollars. They timed it for a weekend in Bangladesh — no one was watching.

The money was routed to the Philippines and laundered through casinos, which were exempt from anti-money-laundering laws at the time.

But one transfer got flagged — not by security software, but because the word "Jupiter" in a routing address matched a sanctioned entity. That single word stopped $850 million from being stolen.

The attackers were traced to North Korea's Lazarus Group.

81 million gone. A typo saved the rest.
