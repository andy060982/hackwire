# SolarWinds — The Biggest Supply Chain Attack in History

## Title
How Russian Hackers Got Inside 18,000 Organizations at Once

## Hashtags

## Script

In December 2020, the cybersecurity world discovered the most devastating supply chain attack in history. Russian hackers had been inside the U.S. government for nine months — and nobody knew.

Instead of attacking targets directly, they compromised SolarWinds — a company whose network monitoring software was used by 33,000 organizations, including the Pentagon and Microsoft.

The hackers injected malicious code into SolarWinds' software build process. When a routine update shipped, 18,000 organizations installed the backdoor themselves. It was signed, trusted, and looked completely legitimate.

They accessed emails at the Treasury Department, Homeland Security, and the Justice Department. They got into Microsoft's source code.

How did they get caught? They hacked FireEye — one of the top cybersecurity firms in the world — and FireEye noticed their own hacking tools had been stolen.

The operation was attributed to Russia's SVR intelligence service.

One software update. 18,000 victims. Nine months undetected.
