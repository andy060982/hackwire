# Kevin Mitnick — The FBI's Most Wanted Hacker

## Title
The Hacker Who Made the FBI Look Foolish for Years

## Script

In the 1990s, the FBI's most wanted computer criminal was a fast-talking hacker from LA named Kevin Mitnick.

His superpower wasn't technical — it was social engineering. He'd call a phone company, pretend to be an employee, and talk his way into getting passwords. He once got Motorola's source code just by asking nicely.

He hacked Nokia, Fujitsu, Sun Microsystems. He cloned cell phones. He even wiretapped the FBI agents chasing him.

For two and a half years, Mitnick was a fugitive using fake identities and burner phones. What brought him down was another hacker — Tsutomu Shimomura, whose computer Mitnick had broken into. Shimomura helped the FBI trace his cell signal to an apartment in North Carolina.

Mitnick spent five years in prison — eight months in solitary because a judge believed he could launch nukes by whistling into a phone.

After prison, he became one of the most respected cybersecurity consultants in the world. He passed away in 2023 — a legend.
