# Stuxnet — The World's First Cyber Weapon

## Title
The Virus That Destroyed Iran's Nuclear Program

## Script

In 2010, researchers discovered the most sophisticated malware ever created. It wasn't designed to steal data. It was designed to physically destroy equipment.

It was called Stuxnet.

The target was Iran's nuclear facility. The virus spread through USB drives because the facility had no internet connection.

Once inside, Stuxnet took control of the centrifuges enriching uranium and made them spin at the wrong speeds. But here's the genius part — it fed fake "everything is normal" data back to the operators. Engineers saw nothing wrong while the centrifuges tore themselves apart.

Over two years, it destroyed a thousand centrifuges — one-fifth of Iran's capacity. It set their nuclear program back by years.

It was a joint U.S.-Israel operation. The first time a cyberattack caused physical destruction in the real world.

Code became a weapon. And warfare changed forever.
